AI doesn't know anything. It guesses the next word.

Tools like ChatGPT and Claude are trained on huge amounts of text. First they learn to predict the next word.

When you ask a question, the model writes the most likely answer, one small piece at a time.

That's why it can sound completely sure, and still be wrong. People call that a hallucination.

So when it gives you a name, a number or a link, check it.

Use AI to draft. You do the checking.
