## Facts found so far (all need a second check on the original page before they go in the script)
- NASA HLS presentation, IAC 2026 (NTRS 20260009199): lists "Starship long-duration orbital test + Starship large-scale cryogenic propellant transfer test" as other major HLS milestones, so the ship-to-ship transfer was not complete at that point.
- NASA OIG report IG-26-004 (March 2026), HLS contracts: the large-scale vehicle-to-vehicle transfer test slipped from March 2025 to March 2026; Artemis III moved to no later than June 2027 (as the report states it).
- SpaceX update: a two-flight plan, one Starship stays in orbit, a second launches and transfers propellant to it, targeted for 2026 depending on Starship V3 progress. A Starship flight has done an internal transfer (liquid oxygen from a header tank to the main tank); that is not ship-to-ship.
- NASA/SpaceX illustration page: "NASA, SpaceX Illustrate Key Moments of Artemis Lunar Lander Mission" (images for the video).
