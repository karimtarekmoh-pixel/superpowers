In 2027, four astronauts will float into a Moon lander about 273 miles above Earth. It won't be on the Moon. This is Artemis III, NASA's Earth-orbit test before the landing, and here is exactly what it tests.

WHAT THE VIDEO COVERS
- Artemis III: launching in 2027, four astronauts, a planned 14-day mission in low Earth orbit. NASA says it will not land on the Moon.
- The sequence: a Blue Moon Mark 2 test article, then Orion on SLS, a docking and crew inspection, then a Starship V3 test article.
- Two different docking designs flown in one mission.
- The schedule puzzle: NASA's published plan does not say what a problem in orbit would do to the 2028 target.
- Why NASA has not yet chosen which lander company goes first to the surface.
- How the Starship and Blue Origin landing plans are meant to work for Artemis IV.
